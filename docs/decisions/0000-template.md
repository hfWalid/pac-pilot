# ADR-0000 — Title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced the decision. The constraint, the pressure, the thing that would go wrong without one.
Enough that a reader in two years understands the situation without reconstructing it.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice. One paragraph.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it forecloses. Include the costs — an ADR that
only lists benefits is advocacy, not a record.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is usually the most valuable section: it is what
stops the same option being re-proposed every six months.
